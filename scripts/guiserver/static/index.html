Pager Control Panel

--

WiFi Master

One switch to silence (or restore) Management/Open/WPA APs, the WiFi client, and recon hopping.


  


  

    
Evil Twin (Clone AP)

    
Uses official WIFI_WPA_AP / WIFI_OPEN_AP. Uplink auto-detects Ethernet vs WiFi client.

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

  


  

    
LAN Scan

    
Wraps nmap over the eth1 (USB-C) interface.

    

      
      
    

    

  


  

    
LAN Kill (DeadNet)

    
Not available from the Control Panel on purpose - ARP-poisoning the whole LAN deserves the CLI's discovery-first flow (see who's actually there before you commit), not a blind one-click button. Use SSH: deadnet --discover then deadnet, or the DeadNet LAN Kill payload on-device.

    

  


  

    
LAN Sniffer

    
Checks which of the two wired PC links (USB-C / USB-A) are connected, then captures + auto-summarizes traffic on one of them.

    

      
    

    

      
      
    

    

      
      
      
    

    

  


  

    
PC Link

    
Detects whatever PC is directly wired in (USB-C tether or USB-A adapter) and captures+summarizes its traffic.

    

      
    

    

      
    

    

      
    

    

  


  

    
Bluetooth

    
No official Hak5 Bluetooth commands exist for the Pager - this drives the real BlueZ stack (hcitool/l2ping/btmgmt) directly. A target already connected+streaming to its real owner usually refuses new connections outright - flood/jam report "NOT REACHABLE" plainly when that happens, not silent nothing.

    

      
    

    

      
    

    

      
      
      
      
      
    

    

  


  

    
WiFi Deauth

    
Runs continuously (repeat deauth every couple seconds) until you press Stop. Authorized network only!
