## WiFi offense
  [W1] WPA handshake capture + crack  [loud,keys]
        Auto-pick the busiest target, force reassociation, capture the 4-way handshake, export to hashcat/hcxtools, crack on-box or offload to a GPU rig.
  [W2] PMKID clientless capture  [stealth,keys]
        Pull WPA key material straight from APs with PMKID enabled — no client present, no deauth needed. Quiet.
  [W3] Karma / known-beacon twin  [loud,creds]
        Listen for probe requests, then become whatever SSID the client is calling for so it auto-associates to you.
  [W5] WPS Pixie-Dust  [loud,keys]
        Offline WPS PIN recovery against vulnerable APs → recover the full PSK without a handshake.
  [W6] Targeted deauth toolkit  [loud,disruptive]
        One client, a whole AP, or band-wide. Timed bursts or sustained. The precision tool behind half the attacks above.
  [W7] SSID beacon flood  [loud,disruptive]
        Spam hundreds of fake SSIDs (your ssidpool) to bury the airwaves and stress clients/scanners.
  [W8] Client-isolation tester  [access]
        On a guest/corp network, prove whether connected clients can actually reach each other — a very common misconfig.
  [W9] PMF / 802.11w posture test  [intel]
        Find out whether management-frame protection actually blocks your deauth — tells you which APs are soft.
  [W12] Rogue 5GHz twin  [stealth,creds]
        Many clients prefer 5GHz and defenders watch it less — stand the twin up there for a quieter catch.
  [W13] Client OS fingerprint (air)  [stealth,intel]
        Guess each client's device/OS from probe-request IE fingerprints without ever associating — target selection intel.

## Wired LAN via USB-A adapter — auto-pwn, not just scan
  [L1] Auto-pwn on plug-in  [loud,access]
        Plug in and walk away: recon → find defaults/known-vuln → exploit → dump loot to file. True Shark-Jack attack mode, not just enumeration.
  [L2] Responder + NTLM relay  [loud,access,hashes]
        Poison LLMNR/NBNS/mDNS, capture NTLMv2, and relay it live to a reachable SMB/HTTP host to land code execution — the classic internal-AD one-two.
  [L3] Default-cred auto-login + config exfil  [creds]
        Walk every router/camera/NAS/printer, log in with known defaults, pull configs and stored credentials.
  [L4] SMB share auto-loot  [creds]
        Crawl every readable share, grep for passwords/keys/configs, and pull the hits into the loot bag.
  [L5] Selective MITM + inject  [loud,MITM]
        Rogue DHCP/ARP to intercept one chosen host and inject a payload/hook into its HTTP traffic.
  [L6] Pivot + reverse tunnel  [access]
        From the wired foothold, punch an egress channel and tunnel back over the mgmt radio for remote access to the target LAN.
  [L7] AD recon + Kerberoast  [hashes]
        If a domain is reachable, enumerate it and request service tickets for offline cracking.
  [L8] Printer / IoT exploitation  [access]
        Known-CVE exploitation of the soft targets everyone forgets — printers, cameras, IoT bridges.
  [L9] Egress-buster + covert exfil  [intel]
        Map what actually escapes the network (DNS/ICMP/odd ports) and stand up a covert exfil channel over it.
  [L10] Deep scan → exploit shortlist  [intel]
        Full service/version scan, auto-matched to a ready-to-fire exploit shortlist per host.
